# 8. Release Plan for Mixes or Tracks

For each new mix, track, or catalogue push, use a simple 3-phase rollout.

## Phase 1: Pre-Promotion

Timeline: 5–7 days before release

Content:

- Teaser clip
- Track/mix mood description
- Behind-the-scenes post
- Story countdown
- “New mix coming soon” post

## Phase 2: Release

Timeline: Release day

Content:

- Main announcement post
- Reel with audio clip
- Story link
- Pin the post temporarily
- Send to close contacts, collaborators, and event organizers

Caption structure:

> New mix is live.  
> Built around [sound/mood/theme].  
> Listen now through the link in bio.

## Phase 3: Post-Release

Timeline: 1–2 weeks after release

Content:

- Highlight 2–3 moments from the mix
- Share listener feedback
- Post a short breakdown of the sound
- Connect it to booking

Example CTA:

> If this is the kind of energy your event needs, booking inquiries are open.

# 9. Booking Promotion Plan

## Monthly Booking Post

Once per month, post a direct booking announcement.

Example:

> Booking is open for upcoming events. Dai is available for queer-centered nights, pop-ups, markets, creative showcases, private events, and community gatherings. If you are looking for an eclectic, intentional set rooted in underground sound and connection, send an inquiry.

## Weekly Booking Reminder

Use stories weekly:

- “Booking inquiries open”
- “Available for upcoming sets”
- “Planning an event? Let’s talk sound.”
- “Book Dai for your next pop-up, party, or community gathering.”

## Booking Assets Needed

Create:

- Booking inquiry form
- Short DJ bio
- Press photo
- 2–3 mix links
- Event type list
- Simple rate sheet or “rates available upon inquiry”
- Past event photos/clips
- Testimonials if available

# 10. Community Outreach Plan

Social media should be paired with direct outreach.

## Who to Reach Out To

- Queer bars and nightlife organizers
- Local art spaces
- Vendor markets
- Community centers
- LGBTQ+ organizations
- Wellness event organizers
- Small businesses
- College/community event boards
- Independent venues
- Underground music collectives

## Outreach Message Structure

Keep it simple:

1. Introduce Dai
2. Explain the music style
3. Name the type of event fit
4. Include listening link
5. Ask about future booking opportunities

Example:

> Hi [Name], I’m Dai, an eclectic DJ, producer, and music educator creating sets rooted in underground sound, queer culture, and community. I’d love to be considered for future events, pop-ups, or community-centered gatherings you’re planning. Here’s a link to my mixes: [link]. Would love to connect if the sound feels aligned.

# 11. How Sweet Glen’s and Transcend Fit Into Music Promotion

These should support the music brand without taking over.

## Sweet Glen’s Tie-In

Use Sweet Glen’s when promoting:

- Pop-ups
- Markets
- Wellness events
- Community gatherings
- Food/vendor collaborations

Example crossover:

> Sound and nourishment in the same space. Dai will be playing a set while Sweet Glen’s shares whole-food drinks rooted in wellness and community care.

## Transcend Tie-In

Use Transcend when promoting:

- Queer-centered events
- Trans resource events
- Community fundraisers
- Affirming creative spaces
- Educational events

Example crossover:

> This set is rooted in affirmation, visibility, and care. Transcend will also be sharing resources for trans community support.

Use this line when tying it all together:

> Music is the center, but community connects everything Dai creates.

# Best First Move

Start with three pinned posts:

1. **Book Dai**  
    Clear explanation of event types, music style, and booking CTA.
2. **Listen to Dai**  
    Links to mixes, original work, playlists, or catalogue.
3. **Music + Community Ecosystem**  
    Short explanation of how Dai’s music connects to Sweet Glen’s, Transcend, and broader community care.

This makes the profile immediately understandable: people can listen, book, and understand the deeper mission.
