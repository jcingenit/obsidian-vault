# General Music Promotion Plan for Dai
# Content Pillars
## #1: Listen to Dai
Post types:
- Mix drops
- Original track announcements
- “Listen now” posts
- Short audio clips
- Playlist shares
- Catalogue highlights

Example caption:
New mix is up. This one moves through underground textures, queer club energy, and sounds I’ve been sitting with lately. Listen, save, and send it to someone who needs something new.

CTA:
Listen through the link in bio.

## #2: Book Dai
Post types:
- “Now booking” posts
- Event type carousels
- Past event recaps
- Testimonials
- Availability updates
- Booking process posts
- “What I bring to a set” posts

Example caption:
Booking is open for pop-ups, private events, queer-centered nights, creative showcases, and community gatherings. If your event needs intentional sound, movement, and energy, let’s connect.

CTA:
Request a booking through the link in bio.

# 6. Weekly Posting Schedule

## Recommended

| Day       | Content                                    | Goal                      |
| --------- | ------------------------------------------ | ------------------------- |
| Monday    | Music discovery post                       | Build taste and authority |
| Tuesday   | Behind-the-scenes or personal reflection   | Build connection          |
| Wednesday | Booking-focused post                       | Generate inquiries        |
| Thursday  | Artist/community spotlight                 | Support ecosystem values  |
| Friday    | Mix clip, event promo, or reel             | Drive energy and listens  |
| Weekend   | Stories from events/listening/current work | Stay visible              |

## Minimal Version

If posting often feels overwhelming, use this simplified version:

**PER WEEK:**
- **2 feed posts**
- **1 reel**
- **3–5 story updates**
- **1 booking reminder**
- **1 music discovery post**
